# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Professional-services firms and compliance teams that need to understand and operate AML, KYC and risk workflows in Microsoft 365. Visitors are evaluating Finperiti's credibility, capabilities and commercial fit.

## Product Purpose

Present Finperiti's compliance products clearly, build confidence in their rigor and usability, and help qualified visitors understand the offering, estimate pricing and make contact.

## Brand Personality

Assured, precise and approachable. The experience should feel expert and dependable without becoming institutional or intimidating.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic fintech templates, alarmist compliance imagery, decorative complexity, inaccessible status indicators and visual treatments that obscure the content or undermine trust.

## Design Principles

- Make complex compliance concepts immediately understandable.
- Communicate expert confidence through evidence and clarity.
- Preserve the established Finperiti visual identity and component language.
- Use functional color consistently and never as the only carrier of meaning.
- Keep every interaction fast, calm and accessible.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain WCAG AA contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, reduced-motion support and layouts that work without JavaScript. Status and risk information must retain explicit text labels so it remains understandable without color perception.
