privacy@finperiti.com

This notice explains how finperiti Limited (“finperiti”, “we”, “us”) collects and uses personal data when you visit finperiti.com, contact us, or ask about our products. It is written to meet our obligations under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the Irish Data Protection Acts 1988–2018 and, for visitors in the UK, the UK GDPR.

1. Who we are

finperiti Limited is the controller of the personal data described in this notice. We are a private company limited by shares, registered in Ireland under company number 756469, and based in Dublin, Ireland.

For any question about this notice or your personal data, email privacy@finperiti.com.

2. What this notice covers

This notice covers our website and the enquiries we receive through it, by email, or at events. It does not cover data that our customers upload to the finperiti platform about their own clients (for example, identity documents collected during onboarding). For that data, our customer is the controller and finperiti processes it only on their instructions under a data processing agreement. If you are a client of one of our customers, please see that firm’s privacy notice.

3. What we collect, why, and our legal basis

What we collect Why we use it Legal basis
Your name, email address, company and message when you use our contact form or email us, and your email address, firm name and the figures you entered when you ask us to email you a pricing calculator estimate To reply to you, arrange a demo, or prepare a quote Taking steps at your request before entering a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f))
Technical data our website host records when your browser requests a page, such as your IP address, browser type and the page requested To deliver the website securely and protect it against misuse Our legitimate interest in running a secure website (Art. 6(1)(f))
Your business contact details and the history of our conversations with you To follow up on your enquiry and, where you are a business contact, to tell you about relevant finperiti products and events Our legitimate interest in developing business relationships (Art. 6(1)(f)), or your consent where the law requires it, for example when you tick the product updates box on our pricing calculators. You can opt out of marketing or withdraw consent at any time.

You do not have to give us your details, but we will not be able to respond to an enquiry without a way to contact you.

4. Cookies

Our website does not use cookies, analytics tools, or similar tracking technologies. If that changes, we will update this notice and ask for your consent before setting any non-essential cookies.

5. Who we share your data with

We share personal data only with organisations that help us run our business:

  • GitHub, Inc. (United States), which hosts our website
  • A form-processing provider, which delivers contact form submissions to our inbox
  • Microsoft Ireland Operations Ltd, which provides our email and business software
  • Our professional advisers, such as lawyers and accountants, where necessary
  • Public authorities, regulators or courts where the law requires us to, or to establish, exercise or defend legal claims
  • A buyer or investor, if all or part of our business is sold or restructured

We do not sell personal data.

6. Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States and India. Where this happens, we rely on an adequacy decision of the European Commission (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, for providers certified under it) or on the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses. You can ask us for more information about these safeguards.

7. How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries that do not lead to a contract: up to 24 months after our last contact with you
  • Enquiries that lead to a contract: for the length of the contract and as long afterwards as the law requires
  • Website technical logs: for the period set by our hosting provider, which we do not control
  • Contact form submissions: our form-processing provider keeps a copy for up to three years, after which it is deleted automatically

8. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data erased
  • Restrict how we use your data
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Object to our use of your data based on legitimate interests, and object at any time to direct marketing
  • Withdraw consent at any time, where we rely on it

To exercise any of these rights, email privacy@finperiti.com. We will respond within one month. We do not make decisions about website visitors based solely on automated processing.

You also have the right to complain to the Data Protection Commission, 21 Fitzwilliam Square South, Dublin 2, D02 RD28, Ireland (dataprotection.ie). If you are in the UK, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). We would welcome the chance to address your concern first.

9. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data, including encryption in transit and restricted access. No system is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will act promptly if an incident occurs.

10. Children

Our website and services are for businesses. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18.

11. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page.
