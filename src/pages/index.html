Why now

Enforcement has reached small firms

AML rules aren’t new. What’s changing is how firmly they’re enforced, and how little room is left for spreadsheets and email.

  1. Today · UK

    The rules already apply

    The Money Laundering Regulations 2017 already require a documented, risk-based approach to client due diligence, with records you can show a supervisor.

  2. July 2027 · EU

    One rulebook, directly applicable

    The EU AML Regulation (2024/1624) applies directly in every member state. One standard, far less national discretion.

  3. AMLA · EU-wide

    Supervision gets coordinated

    The new EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority coordinates national supervisors, so expectations become consistent across borders.
