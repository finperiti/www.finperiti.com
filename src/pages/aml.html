Questions

AML onboarding, answered

What is AML onboarding software?

It replaces the email, spreadsheets and shared folders many firms use to take on new clients. It collects client information and documents, runs identity, company, sanctions, PEP and adverse-media checks, records a risk rating and decision, and keeps an audit trail you can show a supervisor.

Do accountants and other small firms need AML checks?

Yes. Accountants, car dealerships, tax advisers, legal professionals, estate agents and company service providers all have anti-money laundering duties. In the UK the Money Laundering Regulations 2017 already require a documented, risk-based approach to client due diligence, and from July 2027 the EU AML Regulation (2024/1624) applies directly across the EU.

What changes under the EU AML rules in July 2027?

The EU AML Regulation (2024/1624) applies directly in every member state from July 2027, replacing much of the national variation. The new EU Anti-Money Laundering Authority (AMLA) coordinates national supervisors. Under the accompanying directive, maximum fines for non-financial firms such as accountants, tax advisers and estate agents must be at least €1M.

Does finperiti AML work with Microsoft 365?

Yes. Your team signs in with the Microsoft 365 accounts they already use, with no new logins and no IT project. On the Professional plan, your document vault sits in your firm’s own Microsoft 365 tenant.

What checks does finperiti AML run?

KYC on individuals, KYB on companies and their beneficial owners, identity verification with liveness checks, and sanctions, PEP and adverse-media screening. Depending on your plan, checks run manually or automatically, and list changes are tracked over time.

Does the AI make the compliance decision?

No. AI drafts a risk rating and flags issues, but its scores are advisory. An analyst in your firm confirms or overrides each one, and that decision is recorded in the audit trail.

Where is my data stored?

On Essentials, the platform and your documents are hosted and managed by finperiti. On Professional, your document vault sits in your firm’s own Microsoft 365 tenant, with Binarii Labs split-cloud storage so no single provider holds a whole file.

Can we move from our current system?

Yes. Migration from your existing system is included, so your client history is imported rather than re-keyed.
